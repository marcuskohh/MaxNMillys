About us and our History

This fried food joint started in a simple backwater home kitchen with a mother and son experimenting different variations where food can be cooked in. All the way back to 1983 in.

After months of experimenting, the duo came to the solution that fried food was what tasted best to them. Slowly they began cooking more fried food for themselves before slowly sharing with neighbours to share with. After months came the idea of selling fried food from their houses windows to make this passion profitable and gain a wider audience.

Soon after years of selling food from their home window, they had finally saved up enough to rent out an open space to finally open up their own resteraunt which is what you all know today as Max'N'Millys fried chicken.

It has taken alot of hard work and perseverance through silent struggles but here we are bringing to you the taste of fried chicken and fried food made with great love and care.
